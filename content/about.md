## Hello, I'm Venkat Varagani

Welcome to **Bytes & Blessings** — a space where technology meets tradition.

I'm a **Principal Member of Technical Staff (PMTS) at Salesforce**, based in India, with close to two decades of experience building software that scales. Over the years I've worked across domains like system security, payments, finance, and e-commerce — from writing low-level OS components to designing cloud-native microservices.

My journey has taken me through some incredible teams: starting at **Wipro**, then spending over four years at **Intel Security (McAfee)** working on system-level security, followed by a decade at **Intuit** where I rose from Staff Engineer to Principal Engineer building products like **QuickBooks**. Today at Salesforce I work with **Go, Kubernetes, and Apache Kafka**, leading technical initiatives and solving hard distributed systems problems.

I hold a **Master's degree in Computer Science** from Osmania University and am an **AWS Solutions Architect Associate**.

### Why This Blog?

Code is my craft, but it's not the whole picture. I'm equally drawn to ancient temples, Vedic wisdom, and the kind of quiet reflection that gives life its meaning. **Bytes & Blessings** is where these two worlds meet:

**Bytes** — software architecture, Go, microservices, Kubernetes, cloud engineering, and the everyday craft of building resilient systems.

**Blessings** — Sanatana Dharma, temple pilgrimages across South India, and the timeless life lessons woven through our traditions.

Whether you're here for a deep-dive on service-oriented architecture or a story from a temple trail in Tamil Nadu, I hope you find something that resonates.
