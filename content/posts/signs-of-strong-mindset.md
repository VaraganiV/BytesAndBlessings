## 

* Move on, You don't waste time feeling sorry for yourself
* Embrace change; you welcome challenges
* Stay happy. You don't waste energy on things you can't control
* Kind, fair, and unafraid to speak up
* Willing to take calculated risks
* Celebrate other people's success. You don't feel threatened by others' achievements.
