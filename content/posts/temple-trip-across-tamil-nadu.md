# Temple Trip Across Tamil Nadu

Four days, dozens of temples, tons of blessings, one awesome driver, some chaos, and a whole lot of peace.

📅 April 24–27, 2025
📍 Trichy → Kumbakonam → Chidambaram → Srirangam
⏱ 8 min read

TL;DR

A 4-day temple-hopping adventure across Tamil Nadu — from a rare Brahma temple in Trichy to completing all five Panchabhoota Sthalams at Jambukeshwarar, with an unforgettable Spatika Lingam abhishekam at Chidambaram in between. Featuring the best driver in Tamil Nadu, spiritual cardio at sacred trees, and one very questionable plate of fried veggies.

| Day | Route | Temple Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Hyderabad → Trichy | Brahmapureeswarar, Samayapuram Amman |
| Day 2 | Trichy → Salem → Kumbakonam | Murugan, Bhuvarahaswamy, Sarangapani, Nageshwara, Adi Kumbeshwarar |
| Day 3 | Kumbakonam circuit | Suriyanar Koil (Navagraha), Abirami Amman, Uthirapasupatheeshwarar |
| Day 4 | Kumbakonam → Chidambaram → Srirangam → Trichy | Thoniappar, Chidambaram, Jambukeshwarar, Sri Ranganathaswamy |

---

Day
1

## From Hyderabad to Trichy & A Brahma Temple

Hyderabad ✈ Trichy → Tirupattur → Samayapuram

Started from Hyderabad around 3:45 PM. The Indigo flight was (surprisingly) delayed by 20 minutes — but nothing too bad. Reached Trichy by 5:50 PM.

Our driver **Raja** — who turned out to be an absolute gem throughout this trip — picked us up, and we drove straight to **Tirupattur Brahmapureeswarar Temple**. This one was special — my first Brahma temple visit ever. It also has Lord Shiva, and the story goes that Brahma got his destiny rewritten here, and now he does the same for others who visit.

My first-ever Brahma temple visit — where legend says Brahma himself had his destiny rewritten. Now he does the same for devotees who come here.

On the way back, Raja suggested we stop by **Samayapuram Amman Temple** — super powerful vibes, the kind where you just know the deity isn't kidding around. Landed back in Trichy and crashed early. Good start.

---

Day
2

## Heat, Unexpected Temples & Fried Veggies Gone Wrong

Trichy → Illuppili → Salem → Shreemushnam → Kumbakonam

Kicked off the day with **Seergalinathar Temple** in Illuppili — not planned, but somehow we ended up there. Calm place.

Next stop: **Lord Murugan Temple** in Puthiragoundampalayam, Salem district. We reached at noon — peak heat! Felt like we were being slow-roasted, but darshan was totally worth it.

Then came something unique: **Shreemushnam Bhuvarahaswamy Temple** — my first time seeing a **Varaha (boar) form of Vishnu**. Right next to it is a sacred tree temple where we did **24 parikramas** — spiritual cardio at its finest 😄.

#### 🛕 Evening in Kumbakonam — Temples Visited

* Sarangapani Temple
* Nageshwara Temple
* Adi Kumbeshwarar Temple

Back at the hotel, ordered **"Veg Crispy Fry"** — turned out to be over-fried pakoda with random veggies (Dondakaya? Really?). Let's just say I've had better. Lesson learned: stick to the dosa.

---

Day
3

## A Sun Temple Surprise & A Lot of Archana

Kumbakonam → Suriyanar Koil → Thiruchengattangudi

Started early and drove to **Suriyanar Koil (Sun Temple)**. We only planned for Sun god abhishekam, but somehow got pulled into full-on **Navagraha abhishekams** — from Ganesh to Jupiter to Sun. Never seen that before.

(PS: The guy helping us charged more than expected — classic temple moment. You nod, you pay, you move on.)

#### 🛕 Temples Visited

* Suriyanar Koil (Sun Temple + all 9 Navagrahas)
* Sri Thiruvidaikazi Murugan Temple
* Sri Amirthakadeswarar Temple
* Abirami Amman Temple
* Uthirapasupatheeshwarar Swamy Temple, Thiruchengattangudi

At **Abirami Amman Temple**, we were rushing thinking it would close by 12, but it stayed open till 1 PM. Did archana for Shiva, Kalasamhara Murthy, and Abirami Amman. There were so many people doing **Sashti Poorthi** — gave the place a different, emotional vibe.

At Uthirapasupatheeshwarar Temple, prasadam was scheduled for the next day — but we convinced the admin to courier it home. Raja magic strikes again.

Took a break at Le Royale Palace, had lunch, quick nap. Evening drive to Thiruchengattangudi — beautiful old temple with deep history. Back by 8:30 PM, dinner, lights out.

---

Day
4

## Chidambaram Bliss & Completing the Panchabhoota Circuit

Kumbakonam → Sirkazhi → Chidambaram → Srirangam → Trichy ✈

No breakfast today — we just grabbed some **Kumbakonam Degree Coffee** on the go. It was sweeter than expected, but hey, checklist complete! ☕

First stop: **Thoniappar Temple, Sirkazhi**. Super unique structure — Shiva, Amman, and their son together, and a Bhairava shrine on top. Peaceful and beautifully layered temple.

Then came the highlight of the entire trip: **Chidambaram**.

The moment I entered Chidambaram, they were doing abhishekam for the Spatika Lingam. I couldn't believe my luck — this was a dream moment. Stayed through the aarti, got a ₹100 ticket, and went deeper inside. It was dark, but I had a clear darshan of Shiva's feet and his iconic raised hand pose. Goosebumps.

Quick lunch after that, and while heading back to Trichy, we managed to visit **Jambukeshwarar Temple** — completing my visits to all **five Panchabhoota Sthalams** (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space). Major life milestone!

#### 🏆 Panchabhoota Sthalams — All Five Complete!

* **Earth** — Ekambareswarar, Kanchipuram
* **Water** — Jambukeshwarar, Thiruvanaikaval ✅ today
* **Fire** — Arunachaleswarar, Tiruvannamalai
* **Air** — Kalahasti, Srikalahasti
* **Space** — Nataraja, Chidambaram ✅ today

Also squeezed in **Andal Amman Temple** and finally, **Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple, Srirangam**. It was packed, but we made it in. A kind priest let me stand near the deity for a few moments.

At the **Ranganayaki Thayar shrine**, the priest gave me turmeric and even let me into the Garbhalaya after a little dakshina moment. I recited some **Sri Suktham** verses — felt truly blessed.

Wrapped up by 6:15 PM at Trichy Airport. Took a pic with Raja — my trusted driver, planner, and spiritual route expert for the trip.

### ✦ Key Takeaways

* **Completed all 5 Panchabhoota Sthalams** — a spiritual milestone years in the making, wrapped up at Jambukeshwarar and Chidambaram on the same day.
* **A good driver makes the pilgrimage.** Raja wasn't just a driver — he was a planner, a route optimizer, and a temple insider. If you're doing Tamil Nadu temples, invest in a knowledgeable local driver.
* **Leave room for the unplanned.** The best moments — the Spatika Lingam abhishekam, the full Navagraha detour, the Garbhalaya entry at Srirangam — were none of them planned.
* **Kumbakonam Degree Coffee** is sweeter than you'd expect. But you have to try it at least once.
* **Skip the "Veg Crispy Fry"** — trust me on this one. 😄
