## Good Habits that energise you

1. Get comfortable being alone
2. Invest in self-education
3. Master self-disciple
4. Feed your mind with Books, Courses etc
5. Say ‘YES’ even when you’re uncomfortable

## Bad Habits that drain your energy

1. Overthinking
2. Inconsistent sleep
3. Surrounded by negative people
4. Not being physically active
5. Holding anger/resentment
