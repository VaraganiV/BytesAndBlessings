## What Is Thought Detox?

**Detoxing your thoughts** provides a practical technique for dealing with negative thoughts, uncomfortable feelings, and other mental traps to improve total well-being. The following titbits will benefit anyone struggling with self-doubt and lingering, unhealthy thoughts.

**Thoughts are not toxic on their own**; when we give them the power they become dysfunctional. Most people tend to give their thoughts too much power by overidentifying them. When we embrace negative thoughts for too long, they affect how we think about ourselves, our world, and our relationships.

One way to disempower your **dysfunctional thoughts is by self-distancing,** Just take a step back and observe your thoughts like you are not the one having them and believe that just thinking about something doesn't make it a fact.

## Letting Go of Negative Thoughts

**Negative thoughts can be harmless** if we don't permit them to remain. They will persist if they have trapped you into believing they are worth engaging, or you find yourself thinking whether they are true.

The solution to this stickiness is cultivating mindfulness. Mindfulness engages with your experience with curiosity, gentleness, and a non-judgmental attitude rather than mentally checking out or running away. The best way to transform the nature of your thoughts and the hold they have on is by **letting them pass without resistance.**

**Letting go of negative thoughts offers you freedom and helps focus on the present**. The goal is not to avoid negative thoughts but to acknowledge them and no longer give them much attention. Learn to determine which thoughts are worth your time and let go of the dysfunctional and catastrophic ones.

## A Practical Exercise

**Get a paper and write down the thoughts** exactly they come to your mind, fold the paper and keep it somewhere to be dealt with later. Carve out sometime (Worrying time) and ensure that you nudge negative thoughts until then.

> With events that you have zero control over, what would it be like to accept those realities, find beauty or wonder somewhere within them, instead of struggling against them?

You will never get to the point where negative thoughts will completely disappear, but by acknowledging, labeling, and letting them pass away, you will be able to improve your mental health.

**Try this**Write down your definition of happiness and confirm if it brings pleasure and has a sense of purpose attached. Meditate on how you want your life to be summed up when you die. Then write the thoughts down and measure them in place to pursue them.
