Do you feel, you’re struggling to progress in your career? Especially when you have been in the same job for many years – you start to feel stuck.

Do you watch others advance, while you stay in the same position? On the other hand, maybe no one has been promoted for a while. Regardless, there are things you can do. 

## Career Sutras

So, are some key sutras for advancing your career faster?

1. Early in your career, you get paid for what you can do. Later you get paid for what you do.
2. Eventually, every role ends up managing people. Every role !!!!
3. Managing oneself is very different from managing others. Someone who is good at their work does not mean they will become a good manager.
4. You grow professionally by making your boss' life easier.
5. Early on in your career, go deep in whatever you do. Later on in your career, go wide.
6. No one gets rewarded for 'I had the same idea'.
7. The reward for standing by your promised word is immense. Once you have made a commitment, no one should need to follow up on you.
8. Feedback is not given. Feedback is sought. Those who seek it, embrace the truth.
9. If you hustle, you grow fast in the short term. If you pace yourself, you grow big in the long term.
10. Toxic jobs do exist. Where there is no respect or recognition, no progress, no role models for you, no personal space and no/poor financial incentives. Most people are in because they have little or no choice.
11. If you are not happy doing it, you will never be the best at it!
12. Don't be afraid to take a pay cut, for the right experience. That gap will be covered faster than you think it will.
13. Early on, don't say 'it's not my job. Later on, let people do their job.
14. College teaches you and then tests you. Work tests you and then teaches you.
15. Be nice to people. Help people. The bigger the audience who likes you, the more people will take you seriously.
16. Your boss matters! You will learn far more from a competent manager who is strict with you and gives you harsh feedback, than an incompetent manager who is always nice to you.
17. Your network is your opportunity generator. Your colleagues will go on to do wonderfully different things. How you treat them will determine the quality and quantity of opportunities that come through them.
18. You don't find your passion! It is not lying around to be discovered. You grow your passion. By exploring.
