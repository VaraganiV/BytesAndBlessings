## The Beginning

Every developer's journey starts with `Hello, World!` — and so does this blog.

I've been meaning to start writing for years. I have notebooks filled with half-finished thoughts about a tricky Kubernetes bug, the overwhelming peace I felt at Rameshwaram, why the Bhagavad Gita has the best advice on imposter syndrome, and how CI/CD pipelines are oddly similar to daily rituals.

This blog is where all of that finally gets a home.

## Why "Bytes & Blessings"?

**Bytes** represent my professional life — the world of code, data, cloud infrastructure, and AI.

**Blessings** represent my spiritual life — Sanatana Dharma, temple visits, the Vedas, and the deep gratitude I feel for the traditions I grew up with.

The more I learn, the more I realize they're not so different. Debugging teaches patience like meditation. Architecture patterns mirror ancient temple design. Agile development echoes the cycles described in our scriptures.

## What to Expect

- **🖥️ Tech** — Software engineering deep-dives, tutorials, cloud & AI explorations
- **🙏 Sanatana Dharma** — Reflections on scriptures, philosophy, and festivals
- **🛕 Temple Trails** — Stories and guides from temple visits across India and beyond
- **📖 Life Learnings** — Career, spirituality, family, and growth

Here's to new beginnings. 🚀🙏
