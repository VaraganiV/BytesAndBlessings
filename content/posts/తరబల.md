తారాబలం is a strength provided by stars to your Janma / Birth star. Nakshatra/Star of the moon at the time of your birth is known as the Birth star. Moon's daily star / Nakshatra position needs to be favorable for casting any muhurtha. 

The moon takes roughly 27 days to circle around earth and passes through 27 nakshatras. The daily motion of the moon from your janma nakshatra decides this strength. As the moon transits into different stars it becomes favorable or unfavorable to your birth star.  

Tarabalam is divided into three sets of 9 nakshatras. Each part is called Navakam. To calculate tarabalam one must calculate the position of daily nakshatra from your Janma Nakshatra (both nakshatra inclusive). Divide that by 9. If the remainder is 2,4,6,8,9 or 0 then it's good otherwise it's bad.

Janma Nakshatra is the Birth Star of the person and the Nithya Nakshatra is the moon’s start on a particular day. Count the number of nakshatra's between Janma nakshatra to Nithya Nakshatra and divide by the number 9. The reminder will be our star. If the remainder is 2,4,6,8,9 or 0 then it's good otherwise it's bad.

Example

Janma Nakshatra: Ashwini

Nithya Nakshatra: Purva Phalguni

Difference: 11

Divide by 9: 2

Additionally

1. For Star - 1 Janma Tara, if it comes in the first round (diff is less than 9) it is considered bad
2. For Star - 3 Vipath Tara, it is not good if it comes in the second round. (i.e. it is fine if it comes in any other rounds)
3. For Star - 5 Pratk tara, it comes in the third round it's considered bad
4. For Star - 7 Nidhana tara, it's never good.

Remedies

1. If its Janma Tara and we need to initiate some work, donate Vegetables
2. If its Vipath Tara and we need to initiate some work, donate to Jaggery
3. If its Pratk Tara and we need to initiate some work, donate Salt

Nidhana Tara is never good
