Many things differentiate multipliers from diminishers; one of them is what each category thinks about intelligence. Multipliers see intelligence as something that can be cultivated in others. They are comfortable with achieving more by using more of people’s intelligence and capability  

Diminishers are the opposite of multipliers. Their view of intelligence is skewed. They often conclude that other people will never figure things out without them. This egotism makes them think their intelligence is a license to be oppressive and controlling. They find it difficult to trust people to complete a given task and are quick to condemn an individual if they appear to be struggling or failing.

### Multipliers, unlike diminishers:

1. Attract and Optimize talent - Multipliers lead and act around people by helping them discover their talents and putting them to use
2. Create Intensity that requires thinking - Multipliers create a unique and highly motivating work environment that grants everyone the freedom to do their best.
3. Extend challenge - Multipliers operate as challengers by creating more opportunities.
4. Debate decisions - Multipliers allow others to contribute and debate an issue before making decisions.
5. Instill ownership and accountability - Multipliers maintain success by serving as investors; they move people to get committed.

Diminishers are interested only in themselves, while multipliers are interested in others  
Multipliers are leaders who want more individuals to rise, become more intelligent, and become multipliers like themselves. Diminishers are only after building themselves and their space. They’re hardly interested in grooming talents or bringing others to the limelight. There’s genius in everyone, but only multipliers go out of their way to discover raw talents and help them.

Diminishers act like the know-it-alls while multipliers act like challengers

Multipliers push their teams beyond their knowledge as a result they create an organization that deeply understands a challenge and has the focus and energy to face it. On the contrary, diminishers assume their role to be the expert. Multiplier approaches a problem by; first, asking the big intellectual question, and second showing the solution is possible.

Multiplier gives room for debating decisions with the whole team  
Multipliers carry people along in their decision-making processes, encouraging dialogue and debate. They are people persons. Conversely, the diminishers assume that only a few people are worth listening to.

The three practices of multipliers as debate makers include:

1. Understand the issues
2. Sparking the debate
3. Driving sound decisions

### Multipliers can create an organization that can deliver results without their direct involvement

Multipliers' continuous high expectations will rub off on members and make them work towards excellence even without the intervention of the multiplier. When people begin to understand the nature of your involvement as a multiplier, they realize that they hold the majority of the ownership position and the success or failure relies on their efforts

### Try This:

1. Make a list of eight to ten people you work with closely. Note what they do easily
2. Discover the underlying capability that allows them to do some activities excellently
3. Once you’ve developed a hypothesis about each person, test your thinking and refine your views
4. Put your finding to work by listing five roles you could put these persons in the utilize their potential.
