This was a short yet deeply fulfilling road trip through some of Andhra Pradesh’s most spiritual places—**Vijayawada**, **Annavaram**, and **Dwaraka Tirumala**. From early morning poojas to peaceful darshans, tasty roadside snacks to long drives, every moment was memorable. Traveling with Phani added joy to the journey, and visiting temples after years made it all the more special.

### **Day 1: April 3rd – Off to Vijayawada!**

The journey kicked off early morning on April 3rd. We (me and Phani) hit the road from Hyderabad in his trusty Hyundai i10 at around **5:45 AM**. After a calm morning drive, we stopped for breakfast at **7 Restaurant**, then reached **Vijayawada** by **10:45 AM** and checked into **Hotel Manorama**.

We quickly freshened up and headed to the **Durga Amma Temple**. As it was nearing the Nayvedyam break, we waited for about 30 minutes before having a beautiful darshan of **Durga Amma and Sri Durga Malleswara Swamy**. We climbed the temple steps, with Phani applying **turmeric and kumkum** to each one—a lovely devotional gesture.

After returning to the hotel, we had a quick lunch and took a short nap. With nothing planned for the evening, we decided to visit the **Subramanya Swamy Temple** in **Peddanandhipadu**, near my hometown **Varagani**. The darshan was peaceful and special.

Later that night, I had a 9 PM work call with our CTO, which I wrapped up before heading to bed.

### **Day 2: April 4th – Blessed Mornings**

We were up by **3:00 AM** and reached the **Durga Temple** by **3:45 AM**. We were lucky to sit inside the **Garbhalaya** and attend the **Kadgamala Pooja**—a rare and divine experience.

After that, we checked out and drove straight to **Rajahmundry**, reaching by afternoon. We visited **Swaradeep’s house**, greeted his sister, and headed to **Pushkar Ghat** for a holy **Godavari bath**. After the refreshing dip, we found a **mirchi bandi** nearby and relished some **Cut Mirchi**, **Brinjal**, and **Capsicum bajjis**—hot, spicy, and perfect!

From there, we headed towards **Annavaram**, reached by **8 PM**, checked into our room, and had a quick darshan of **Lord Satyanarayana Swamy** before sleeping early.

### **Day 3: April 5th – Vratham, Darshans & Back Home**

We woke at **4:00 AM** to perform the **Satyanarayana Swamy Vratham**. I had pre-booked a ₹2000 ticket, and we were lucky to sit in the **AC hall close to the sanctum**. After nearly **15 years**, performing the pooja together felt really special.

After the pooja, we began our journey to **Dwaraka Tirumala**. On the way, near the giant **Hanuman statue**, I stopped for a quick breakfast—**Idli and Pesarattu** with **chutney and allam pachadi**. It was delicious!

We reached **Dwaraka Tirumala** by **12 noon**, had a quick darshan, and also donated towards **Annadanam** for the next day. Then we began our return to **Vijayawada**.

On the way, we stopped at **Gayatri Brahmana Bojanam**, but the food didn’t meet our expectations. So, we headed to **RedFox Restaurant** near Hotel Manorama for a satisfying meal. After a short evening walk around **Benz Circle** and some light snacks, we called it a day.

The next morning, **April 6th**, we started back to **Hyderabad** at **5:30 AM** and reached by **10:00 AM**, wrapping up this soulful trip with our hearts full and minds refreshed.
