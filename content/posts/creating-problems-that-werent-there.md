The human brain is the most important tool in the human world. It is more significant than any device, and it’s this tool that is responsible for processing your thoughts. Humans are impractical beings, and this makes it important to be able to control thoughts to channel positive energy into them.

Controlling your thoughts is the first step toward a permanent life of sanity. With the right mind-frame, it is possible to be in charge of what you think. Controlling your thoughts is different from controlling your consciousness. Consciousness can’t be controlled. You can’t control what comes to your mind; you can ONLY control what you interpret them to be.

### Training your mind to think straight

Train your mind to be calm and focused. Accept your consciousness for what it is, but train your mind to focus on the important thoughts.

### Understanding thoughts will help you change your life

Thoughts are as important as life itself because it is the platform on which ideas are conceived. Once you grasp the reality of life, your thoughts change for the better and this positivity affects your planning

### Select your thoughts by disregarding thoughts that have NO impact on your life

The brain receives tons of information and it is left for it to filter these thoughts. To get a practical method of filtering your thoughts, ask yourself this question: Will the outcome of a sudden decision change the way I live? If the answer is no then you should disregard such thoughts

### Minimize your thoughts and only think about the things you can control

Thoughts come in varying dimensions, and if you pay enough attention, you’ll realize that most of your thoughts are useless and meaningless. There’s a huge possibility that you are worrying and stressing out yourself by thinking about things that are irrelevant to you. Allow your mind to focus on your desires, actions, words, and intentions. These are the aspects of your life that you need to focus your thoughts on because they are responsible for your being.

### Base your thoughts and perceptions on facts and not assumptions

Facts lead your thoughts instead of assumptions. Fact opens your mind to research, statistics, and experiments that make your knowledge grow in every aspect.

### Think things through; be calm when you face barriers

Most of the time, your first reactions are not always the best; they tend to be outdated and rushed. Thinking through is essential because it gives you time to reflect on your decisions very well before you make them.

### Illustrating your thoughts makes it easier to understand

When you illustrate your thoughts you find it easy to evaluate them, and you even make it easy for the people to follow them.

### You shouldn’t think your way out of everything

Not all thoughts need processing. There are times when you need to let go of all these thoughts by letting your mind go blank.

### Pay attention to details; don’t brood over mistakes

The truth of the matter is that the mistakes have already been made; what you need is a solution to its effects. Dealing with barriers and obstacles doesn’t require “Why” because some mistakes are avoidable by paying attention to details.

### Improve the quality of thoughts

By making assertions instead of asking useless questions, you take more control of your life and choices. When you improve the quality of your thoughts you automatically improve the quality of your actions.

### Make maximum use of your time

The key to using your time well lies in your ability to filter your thoughts. Don’t waste time on thoughts that mean nothing to you. Pick the thoughts that would affect your life, reflect on them and act on them. This way you’ll end up being in control of your time.

**The ability to flow with the tides of life relies heavily on how well you can control your thoughts. Your thoughts are your actions and your actions are what make you. Control your thoughts and you stand a great chance of finding life very easy to live.**
