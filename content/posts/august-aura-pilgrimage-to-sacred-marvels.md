Embracing Shirdi, BhimaShankar, Grishneshwar, and Triambakeshwar

## Day 1: Diving into Nashik's Spiritual Vibe

Our journey began as we alighted at Nagarsole and went to Nashik. Our first stop was the serene Ramkund Ghat, where we immersed ourselves in a refreshing bath. The captivating Kapaleshwar Mandir was next on our list, offering a truly pleasant experience. Next up, we hit Kapaleshwar Mandir – talk about an absolute vibe.

Nashik had us hooked. We went all over the place, doing a mini tour of some cool spots:

* Tapovan – Picture a slice of calm heaven.
* Lakshman's "nope, not today" to Surpanaka – Legendary stuff.
* Lakshmi Narayan Temple – Peaceful vibes galore.
* Ravan's "Oops, I took Sita" spot – History in the making.
* Panchavati – Straight out of a fairy tale.
* Katya Hanuman – Guardian vibes, on point.
* Kalaram – Mind-blowing architecture.
* Kapaleshwar – Major heart-eyes moment.

Our journey continued to Triambakeshwar, though the crowds were thicker due to the Adika Sravanam masam. We patiently waited three hours for the 500 Rs ticket, and our darshan took another three hours. The experience was truly delightful.

By 5 PM, we arrived at Shirdi, swiftly prepared ourselves, and entered the sacred Sai Mandhir. Our darshan was brief yet meaningful, followed by visits to Dwarakamayee, Chavadi, and the inner sanctum of Sai Mandir.

Our first day was a medley of spiritual encounters, historic sites, and shared moments. The serenity of Nashik and the divine presence of Shirdi left an indelible mark on our hearts, promising more wonderful experiences to come.

## Day 2: Dawn to Dusk – Bhima Shankar and Sai Mandhir Blessings

Learning from our previous day's adventure, we embraced an early start at 4 AM, eager to beat the crowds. Our destination? Bhima Shankar, a journey spanning over 180 kilometres from Shirdi.

The road trip was an absolute delight. Raindrops painted the landscape, and the hills flanking us created a picturesque scene. Navigating the Ghat road was a pleasant experience in itself.

By 8:30 AM, we had arrived – greeted by a bustling queue of fellow devotees. Opting for the 500 Rs ticket, we patiently awaited our turn, with distribution starting at 9:30 AM.

A swift darshan followed. And then, a moment of deep devotion – we secured an abhishekam ticket for a divine Panchamrutha Abhishekha within the Grabhalaya. Over 30 minutes in the sanctum, emotions swirling beyond words left us speechless.

Our Bhima Shankar journey concluded by 11 AM, retracing our path to Shirdi. At 4 PM, we were back, satisfying our hunger along the Nashik highway.

As 5:30 PM approached, our next stop was khandoba Mandhir. With perfect timing, we embraced the evening Aarthi and, at Sai Mandhir, witnessed the Aarthi through the Mukha Darshan window. Another day, another cascade of blessings.  

Om Namah Shivaya

## Day 3: Embracing Divine Connections and Cultural Charms

Our day commenced at 6:00 AM, arriving at Sai Mandir by 6:30 AM. At SatyaVratha Mandir Hall, a special call awaited us – participating in seva by arranging flowers used in the morning rituals. A serene and meaningful start to the day.

After performing Satyavratha, we relished a swift mukha darshan. Following breakfast, we bid adieu to our hotel and embarked on a journey to Grishneshwar Temple.

Graced by Sravan masa and the sanctity of Somavaram, we found ourselves amidst the temple's bustling crowd. A three-hour wait rewarded us with a sacred darshan, allowing us precious moments within the Sanctum.

Later, we visited the tranquil Bhadra Maruti Temple, where Lord Hanuman rests in peaceful slumber. A satisfying lunch led us to the fascinating Elora Caves, a leisurely exploration that enriched our day.

A local gem, Jagadhamna Temple, beckoned, offering a serene interlude in the village. Following our cab driver's lead, we indulged in delightful vada pav, jalebi, and a mixture to fuel our journey.

With time on our hands, we delved into Paithani Handloom sarees, indulging in some shopping. Our driver then accompanied us to the train station, marking the conclusion of our unforgettable journey.
