Breathing is a core element of **yoga practice**. Yogic breathing, or pranayama, refers to conscious and controlled breathing that matches the rhythm of yoga postures. There are also breathing exercises people can practice independently of movement

* Right palm on the centre of the chest
* Left palm on belly
* Inhale Slowly, Belly Out, Chest Out
* Exhale Long, Chest In, Belly In

### Perform for 5 min.

### Impacts:

* Strengths respiratory muscles
* Improves digestion, improves lymphatic drainage and circulation
* Develops breath control, and calms nerves.
