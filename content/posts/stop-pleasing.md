True personalities do not try to please people. They don't seek approval from outside. Craving social acceptance is the sign of a person who is in the phase of developing his personality. We all go through this phase at some point in our lives which gives us a sense of achievement. As we keep progressing through this journey of life we all realise that it is impossible to please everybody and neither is there any need to please everybody.

Pleasing people isn't all bad instead it's a sign that you care about others' thoughts and their feelings BUT pleasing people at the cost of your personal life can be costly and may impact your own advancement and growth.

### Why do we engage in pleasing people - FEAR

* Fear of isolation from the social world
* Fear of disapproval from people we love and care
* Fear of criticism from society
* Craving for acceptance

### How to fix:

> WHY's are the answers.

"When we are not trying to please others, we begin to understand what is right for us" -

Byron Katie.
