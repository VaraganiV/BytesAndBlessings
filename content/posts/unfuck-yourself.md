## The Weight of Self-Talk

How willing are you to consider that your current life situation is not a result of your current circumstance but rather the weight of self-talk or internal conversation that pulls you down? Realizing this fact is a positive step in the right direction to helping you get your sensational life.

If human emotions primarily result from thinking, then one may appreciably control one’s feelings by controlling one’s thoughts-or by changing internalized sentences, and self-talk with which one largely created the feeling in the first place.

Willingness is a state that enables you to engage with life and see a situation from a new outlook. Get a complete hold of your thoughts and liven up the willing mind to make great exploits in life. The idea of willingness is that it is the lifeblood of the new, the infinite well of possibility and potential, a state where new futures are birthed and a new you can begin. Hence if you ardently desire to begin a new life to success, be Willing.

## Embrace Uncertainty

Embrace your uncertainties Our obsessions with certainty can be regrettable and ineffective. Uncertainty is where great innovations happen; it is your personal pathway to opportunities. It is the region where you can grow and experience everything new. One reason why many avoid uncertainty is the fear of being judged by others. You can never attain the height of your true potential if you keep wallowing in what others think. In fact, you can get a complete turning point in your life if you ignore the idea that people’s opinion matters. Focus on the things you can control and stop worrying about what you can’t or what anyone thinks about you. Explore your world and tell yourself  “I embrace the uncertainty”

You’re not your thoughts You’re not defined by the internal conversation in your head; you are defined by what you do. The kind of thoughts you groom in your mind largely affects your predisposition to life and your life in its entirety. A positive change in your thought means a positive change in life. “You can change your life by doing or acting not by thinking”

## Expect Nothing, Accept Everything

**Expect nothing, accept everything and live a spectacular life** At times we subconsciously build hidden expectations in our business, career, relationships, and life in general which get in the way of our real lives, issues and items that require our attention. The expectations are like the mirage that diverts us from our true power to take profound and decisive decisions-hence, you end up working on your hidden expectations instead of taking proactive decisions that positively impact your situation.

With no expectations, you’re living in the moment. However, this doesn’t mean you can’t plan but when you make plans deal with whatever outcome the plan produces and don’t shrink yourself into depressive thoughts. You need to love the life your life, not the one you expected to have lived. Your life, success, power to change, and power to let go all lie in your hands. So embrace the potential that is within your reach - make the positive assertion “ I expect nothing and I accept everything”.

We all long to have a beautiful and spectacular life. Claim the greatness that’s yours by putting all the positive assertions above action. Relentlessly pursuing your goals and ultimately succeeding.

Try the following:

1. Do it now
2. Cut out the self-defeating thoughts
3. Control of your mind
4. Separate thoughts from actions
5. Drop worries, excuses, distractions and hidden expectations
6. Love the life you live.
