## The Hidden Treasure Within

If you open your mental eyes and see the treasure house of infinity within you, you will find infinite wealth all
around us. Within you lies a gold mine, from which you may extract whatever you require to live a magnificent,
cheerful, and abundant life

A magnetic piece of steel will lift nearly twelve times its weight, whereas a demagnetized piece of steel will barely
even lift a feather. Similarly, there are two categories of people. There is a magnetic man who exudes confidence
and faith. He is aware that he was born to win and succeed. And then there is the demagnetized man. He feels
dread and uncertainty. When opportunities come, they are concerned that they may fail, lose money, and face
criticism from others. These people will not do well in life.

Discover the ultimate secret of the centuries as a magnetic man.

## Conscious vs Subconscious Mind

Our minds have two distinct personalities. These functions are distinguished as the objective and subjective mind,
the conscious and subconscious mind, and the waking and sleeping mind. Your conscious mind is the captain and
master of your ship, which represents your body, environment, and affairs. Your subconscious mind accepts the
orders you give it based on what your conscious mind believes.

When the subconscious mind accepts a concept, it begins to carry it out. When used negatively, it leads to
failure, dissatisfaction, and unhappiness. When you think constructively, you enjoy health, success, and
prosperity.

By learning to connect and unleash the hidden power of your subconscious mind, you may bring more power,
prosperity, health, happiness, and joy into your life.

Working power of your subconscious

The subconscious mind possesses great power. It inspires, directs, and unveils memories and scenes. It never
sleeps or takes a break.

Simply informing your subconscious before going to bed that you want something done will allow you to connect
to the mind's miraculous powers. You will be overjoyed to learn that the abilities inside you will be released,
resulting in the desired conclusion. That would be the form of energy that pushes the Earth, directs the planet's
orbits, and allows the sun to shine.

Techniques to invoke the subconscious mind

Prayer is the expression of an idea about something we want to accomplish. Prayer is the sincere
desire of the soul. It arises from your wants and shows the things you desire in life.

Visualization Technique: The easiest and most obvious way to develop an idea is to see it as if it were alive in
your subconscious mind. If you stick to your mental image, the concept and thought will eventually develop in
your current world.

The process of thinking creates mental impressions. When your perceptions manifest, they become realities and
events in your life.

Sleeping Technique: The conscious mind is completely submerged when entering a sleepy or drowsy
condition. If you wish to break a bad habit, go to sleep and repeat to yourself, "I am fully free from this habit."
The emotional significance of the words grows with each repetition.

Your prayer is for what you desire. Imagine this fulfillment of desire now and feel it as reality. You can sense the
delight of your prayers.

ThankYou Technique: We make our demands known via praise and thanks. This simple prayer practice has
some great results.
For example, a parent promises his son a bike for graduation. Although the child has not yet received the bike,
he is just as appreciative, happy, and joyful as if he had. Even though he has not yet received it, he is confident
that his father will keep his word.
Saying, "Thank you, father," and expressing his gratitude in his mind will bring his thoughts and emotions to the
point of acceptance. And his affirmation will offer him hope when he is afraid, has no thoughts, or is troubled.

Power of your subconscious for wealth

Wealth is the subconscious conviction of an individual. You will not become a millionaire just by proclaiming that
you are one, but rather by instilling the concept of health and prosperity in your mind. If you are having trouble
making ends meet, it is when you have not convinced your subconscious mind that you will always have plenty
and some to spare

One technique for resolving your mental conflict about wealth is to frequently assert, “I am being prospered in all
of my interests.”

Power of your subconscious for interrelationships

When you radiate love, peace, and happiness to your partner, the subconscious mind picks up on these vibrations,
resulting in mutual trust, affection, and respect. The deeper currents of your subconscious mind will then bring
both of you together in perfect order.

Conclusion

Whatever thoughts, beliefs, opinions, theories, or dogmas you write, engrave, or imprint on your subconscious
mind will manifest objectively as situations, conditions, and occurrences. *What you write inside will emerge on
the outside*.
