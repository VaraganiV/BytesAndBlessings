## Winning Arguments

Some tricks to Win Arguments

* **Confuse** People: One of the best strategies.
* Stare at their **forehead** - slightly above the eye-line
* Stay calm and **Just agree** with them
* Storytelling - Create a compelling **story** using Empathy, Facts and Emotions
* Silence - Stay quiet for a **long time**
