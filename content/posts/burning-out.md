## Signs of Burnout

Are you burning out...

* Feel Discomfort from everything
* Used to be motivated
* Used to be passionate
* Becoming moody and irritable
* Neglecting your self-care
* Additional Changes

+ Losing interest and motivation
+ Feeling detached
+ Neglecting yourself

## Recovery Steps

Try .....

* Make targets feel achievable
* Make success goalposts clearer
* Gain a greater sense of autonomy by making a ‘not-to-do list
* Make work feel more open and supportive
