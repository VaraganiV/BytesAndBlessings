Temples, Travel & Everything In Between

In March 2025, I visited **Pune**, accompanying **Phani** for her exam. While the main reason for the trip was academic, we decided to make the most of our time by exploring a few iconic places around the city.

### A Calm Evening at Chintamani Ganapathy Temple

One of the evenings, we planned a visit to the **Chintamani Ganapathy Temple**, which is one of the famous **Ashta Vinayaka temples** in Maharashtra.

I started from the hotel around **6:20 PM**, picked up Phani from her guide's house, and by **7:40 PM**, we reached the temple. Thankfully, it wasn’t crowded. We had a **pleasant darshan**, spent some peaceful time sitting in the temple premises, and then decided to head back.

Travel-wise, I booked an auto from the hotel to Phani’s guide’s house. The driver kindly agreed to take us through the temple and drop us back at the hotel afterwards, making the commute smooth and hassle-free.

### Early Morning Drive to Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga

Another memorable part of the trip was our early morning visit to **Bhimashankar Temple**, known as one of the twelve **Jyotirlingas**.

To avoid the crowds, I booked a cab the previous day and made sure to remind the driver several times. He was super punctual, showing up at **3:15 AM** (even before the agreed **3:30 AM**), driving a brand-new car, and excited himself as it was his first time visiting Bhimashankar.

We reached by **7:00 AM**. Initially, it was calm but gradually started getting busier. After a smooth darshan, we sat for a while and participated in a **Rudrabhishek** ritual. For about 15 minutes, we could sit close to the **Linga**, performing abhisheka with curd, honey, milk, vibhuti, sandalwood, and kumkum — definitely a serene moment amidst the morning hustle.

An interesting detail about Bhimashankar is that the **Jyotirlinga** represents both **Shiva and Parvati** — the right side symbolizing Shiva, and the left Parvati.

Once done, we made our way back. And, as always, a production issue call came in to add some work drama to the mix! We reached the hotel by **12:30 PM**.

### The Journey Back to Hyderabad

Our trip concluded with a comfortable ride back to Hyderabad on the **Shatabdi Express (E1 coach)**. The service on the train was great — food kept coming in at regular intervals, and the views outside the window made the journey even more enjoyable. We reached Hyderabad by **2:15 PM**, wrapping up what was a short yet refreshing break.

What started as a trip for Phani’s exam turned into an eventful few days filled with temple visits, smooth travels, unexpected work calls, and memorable moments. Pune in March had its own charm — and we managed to experience a good slice of it.
