The best of life is the one where you are fulfilled and happy. You are the pilot of your life, and you’re the one who can decide whether to live a good life or not. The only person that is to account for whatever is happening to you is YOU.

The first step to living a good life is knowing how to live a GOOD LIFE. There are many people who tried different methods on how to grow in life but have failed because of trying the wrong methods or doing things in the wrong way. Living a good life requires having a working plan and the determination to succeed - no matter what.

P4 - the ultimate framework for your life  
Everything you do in life falls under four categories. These four categories shape your life and existence. They shape the way you live, see the world, and approach a situation. The four categories are called P4. P4 represents Power, Passion, Purpose, and Production. Each of these principles represents something unique about your life.

* Power - Body
* Passion - Relationships
* Purpose - Mind
* Production - Business

Your body represents yourself your physical presence. The relationship represents how you interact with everyone around you, your family, and your friends. The mind is concerned about how you view things with positivity and embrace the values of life and your business involves your income and financial security.

This P4 serves as a map as well as a blueprint for you. Its contents are not negotiable in any way because with P4, you can solve every problem you face in life. 

Manage your body is very integral to your growth 

Your body is an enabler of many things you do, having a fit body is not a luxury; it's an absolute necessity. The more you get in shape, the better your body responds to every aspect of your life. Getting fit makes your body sharper and your brain more active than before.

Along with a fit body, there is a need to develop a better Passion (relationship) with everyone you meet. Nothing kills growth more than being in a bad relationship with the people around you. Bad relationships are not limited to the connections; it extends to harboring anger, sadness, depression, evil thoughts, etc. When you are filled with these emotions, you can never grow. 

There’s a limit to the level of control you have over other people. But there’s no limit to how much you can control yourself.

Every single thing you do starts in the mind

Every choice you make, and every step you take is a product of what’s going on in your mind. Your mind is like a garden that will always grow things, but what it grows totally depends on you. If you fail to do nothing, your mind will be clouded with nothing but imagination. Just like a garden, if you fail to take care of it it will grow weeds.

Your mind is your power to make decisions, you can’t control the thoughts that come to your mind, but you can control your reactions to them. you must learn to control your mind by feeding it with good vibes, positivity, and valuable knowledge.

Mediate daily and devote considerable time to studying. Daily studying and meditation help you to relax and concentrate well on many things

Whatever you do as a career is enough to sustain you well

You should get paid every day because if you don’t you’ll get trapped in “brokenness”. Being broke disrupts a lot of things. First, it takes away your freedom to make plans, and then it renders your ideas useless because you do not have enough to implement whatever idea you have.

You must follow the following steps of being more productive

1. Add value to your life by letting people know what you are capable of doing and why they should listen to you.
2. Know what you want, why you want it, and how you want it.
3. Research on your own and learn more.
4. Learn from people and copy models that have produced successful results.

You are responsible for whatever happens in your life

Every aspect of your life is interwoven and connected in one way or the other. This means that damage to one aspect of your life can lead to more damage in all other areas.

The key to becoming better is accepting that you have been doing it the wrong way, this helps you turn to do it the right way

There are several ways you can create happiness for yourself

* Quit your job if it's not making you happy. The best thing to do is to find out which one works for you and make the necessary corrections.
* Stay away from people that will keep drawing you back. Avoid people that bring bad energy to you.
* Approach every day with positivity. Look forward to the next day as a day of productivity.
* Look forward to every challenge with a determination to win. Strive to always win.
* Appreciate more and complain less. When you complain too much, you’ll barely become more productive because it would mean you’re more focused on faults rather than productivity.

Sustaining a good life requires a lot of determination and grit. It requires focus and dedication to your goals,  the P4 teaches you the importance of life and why you must do everything you can to live a healthy lifestyle. You must learn and unlearn a lot of things if you want to stand out and live a fulfilled life.
