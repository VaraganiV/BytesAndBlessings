Many people believe that the main obstacle to a full, successful life is the outside world. This is not true because the most common enemy of success is a person’s ego. Early in careers, our ego impedes learning and the cultivation of talent. With success, it can also blind us to our faults and cause future problems.

### Anything that can impede your journey to success, it’s EGO

Your ego is the enemy of whatever you aspire to be. To aspire is to have a goal, a calling, a new beginning, or set out to do something. But you can’t just have aspirations without first believing that you have the ability to achieve your goals. Your belief in yourself does not depend on achievement or success. It depends on how self-contained, self-motivated, and principled you are. Once they have a particular talent, they have everything they need. But this is not true; you need to constantly evaluate your ability because this creates room for improvement.

A person’s ego can manifest in so many ways, and one of those is when a person talks too much instead of working. Work quietly and ignore the impulse to seek attention through talking. It’s not out of place to be excited and nervous whenever you start something, so we tend to comfort ourselves externally instead of internally. This external comfort involves a lot of talking.

“If you want to overcome your ego, you have to detach yourself from your emotions and do the hard work of self-improvement”

### Nobody knows it all

Being humble you can overcome the assumption that you know it all. Until you realize that you do not know everything, you will be unable to learn. The day you stop learning is the day you die. When we constantly remind ourselves that we are not the best person in the world, we’ll be able to manage our egos.

“There is always someone better than you, someone you can learn from.”

Being a mentee can help you rein in your ego. In talking or receiving feedback, the ego tends to ignore harsh or negative feedback by telling us we do not need them when, in fact, we do. We become defensive. Again the way out of this trap is to be humble.

Another way to avoid the ego trap is by teaching others. Teaching exposes you to a wide range of possibilities in your field.

### 

### Pride prevents you from listening to advice

Pride makes you cling to pass glory. Truly successful people know that they cannot afford to stop learning. They always push harder and try to outdo their last victory. If steve jobs had allowed his achievement with the Apple II computer to get into his head, we probably would not have iPhones and iPads today.

“Not only will pride keep you from improving, but it will also make you resistant to correction”

To overcome pride, ask yourself how a humble person would approach a situation and follow their example.

### You’re only as strong as your humility and self-control

Humility cannot hurt your career,  Appreciating your friends and colleagues, teachers and trainers is a good thing. Nonetheless, you need to respect your fans and well-wishers by thanking them profusely for their support.

As you collaborate with others to achieve success, you also need to learn the art of self-control.

Whatever we desire or aspire to achieve depends on the things we are willing to tolerate. Getting angry, emotional, or losing restraint is a recipe for failure. 

No matter how talented, rich or connected you are, you have to subdue your ego by fighting back. This will save your relationships with others and help you on your way to success.

### Ego is always the enemy of whatever feat you plan to achieve, but sobriety can help you

A great way to maintain ego sobriety is to grow up and organize yourself. You must have the ability to manage yourself and others. Beware of the disease called “ME”. It begins with thinking you’re better or special and that your problems and experiences are different from other people’s problems and experiences. Hence they can’t understand you. This attitude is self fish and prevents you from learning.

Ego is a part of everyone which develops unconsciously. When you succeed at something, you should watch out for the possibility of ego rearing its ugly head. If the untamed ego can lead to catastrophic outcomes in one’s life. It is the enemy in matters of aspiration, success, and failure. When you allow ego to fester, it can prevent you from reaching your potential and living truly, happily, and lovingly.

Your life is the sum of all the decisions you make. By deciding that ego is the enemy, you’re making the right choice. You’re choosing humility, knowledge, and self-control, and these are fundamental to your journey to success.
