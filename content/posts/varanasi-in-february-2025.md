Ghats, Temples & Maha Kumbh Moments

In February 2025, I had the chance to visit **Varanasi**, a city that's always been high on my travel list. What started as a simple plan turned out to be an experience filled with spiritual energy, memorable dips, and even a bit of rural adventure!

### February 19: Arriving in the Holy City

Our journey began early on **19th February**. We left at **5:30 AM** and completed check-in by **7:30 AM**. Before boarding, I grabbed a coffee at Tim Hortons. Honestly, the coffee was pretty average—not the kick I was hoping for!

After a smooth flight, we landed in Varanasi and checked in at the **Andhra Ashramam**. Wasting no time, I headed straight to **Chintamani Ganapati Temple**, followed by a visit to **Kedareshwar Temple**.

Later, we spent some peaceful time sitting by the **banks of the Ganges**. Watching life unfold on the ghats was something else—calm yet vibrant. We wrapped up the evening with a boat ride across the ghats, taking in the beauty of Varanasi from the river.

Dinner was at **Banaras Chaats**, and as expected, the street food didn’t disappoint!

By night, **Sravan and his friends** arrived. We caught up and chalked out plans for the next day.

### February 20: Darshan, Annapurna, and Maha Kumbh at Prayagraj

**Feb 20th** started super early. We woke up at **3:00 AM** and reached **Gate 4** for darshan. The early morning vibes at **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** were truly serene, and we finished our darshan by **7:30 AM** without any rush or crowd.

Next, we visited the **Annapurna Temple**. This was a special experience — I had a **very close darshan**. Just a week earlier, **Sri Vidhushekhara Bharati Swamigal of Sarada Mutt** had installed a new idol of Annapurna Devi and performed abhishekam. Standing so close to the idol, it felt immensely satisfying.

Without wasting much time, we started for **Prayagraj** to attend the **Maha Kumbh**. Our driver, **Sundeep**, played a crucial role here. Spotting a blocked road, he confidently took us through the rural roads—a detour that gave us a peek into village life.

We reached **Kumbh Mela** around **2 PM**, parking the car and walking about **4 km** to reach the **Sangam**. Words can't describe the scale of it—millions of people, yet everything managed smoothly. Kudos to the authorities!

Taking dips at the Sangam felt surreal. We performed **tharpayam** and even took our dips at the same spot where the **Naga Sadhus** had earlier bathed. Truly, a once-in-a-lifetime moment.

On our return, while Sundeep did manage to rub his vehicle against a parked one (just avoiding a collision with a bus!), we laughed it off. We had dinner on the way and called it a night by **11 PM**.

### February 21: Dips, Temples & Quiet Moments

We continued our exploration with a peaceful **boat ride** and then headed straight to **Manikarnika Ghat** for another dip. We timed it perfectly at **12 PM**, which is believed to be an auspicious time—legend says even the gods take a dip at this time in Kashi!

Post that, we visited the **Visalakshi Temple**, returned to the room for a quiet break, and had lunch.

In the evening, we made our way to **Gavalagauri Temple** and then to the **Hanuman Temple**, known as the place where **Hanuman Chalisa** was written. The energy there was something else—raw and powerful.

### February 22: Wrapping Up the Varanasi Journey

On our final day, we started at **4 AM**, visiting **Kala Bhairava Temple**, and ****Bindu Madhav Temple** Temple**.

We then headed to **Vyasa Kashi**, where we visited the spot where **Vyasa Maharshi** is believed to have meditated. Our last stop was the **Kali Temple**, rounding off our temple visits.

With everything almost perfectly on schedule, we made it back to the airport, grabbed lunch, and wrapped up this incredible trip.

This Varanasi trip had it all—**divine moments, cultural richness, unexpected rural detours, and a peek into the grand scale of Maha Kumbh**. Every experience, from the peaceful Ganges boat rides to dips at auspicious times, left a lasting memory.

Varanasi never fails to surprise and satisfy. Grateful for these unforgettable days!
