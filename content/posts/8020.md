There are only a few theories that cut across segments and 80 / 20 is one of them. It's commonly called the “Pareto Principle”. The principle holds that a MINORITY of causes, Inputs, or efforts are responsible for the MAJORITY of the results.

Only a few things are responsible for the vast results we see around us. Just as only a few people control the majority of the world’s wealth, and only a few students come out top in their class. If you take time to observe this principle at work all through life.

### Achieve more in less time

To effectively maximize the 80/20 rule, understand that the imbalance in our world is real and natural and start to imbibe 80/20 thinking.

Ask the most productive people, they aren’t productive 24 hours a day. There are specific times when each of us is highly productive. Put 80/20 thinking for effective time usage and creative ways to leverage time.

How to know your most productive times - think about the last time you felt really productive. Go over at least two more productive days, too just so that the conclusion is certain to work predictably. Then document WHY and WHEN you were productive on those days. DID you work night or early hours, WHAT happened a day before, or WHAT's your state that contributed to this productivity? HOW did you handle distractions on those days. 

Applying 80/20 thinking, you will understand the pattern.

Relationships, Choose Quality Over Quality

We can’t do without relationships. Life isn’t meant to be lived in isolation, We would always need people by our sides. It’s pride to say you don't need anyone.

“Choose your relationships wisely”

You can’t choose your family members but you have the power to choose every other person you hang around. It’s in your power to choose your significant other, your best friend, your allies, neighbors and so … make it count.

We have limited space for close relationships. You can be friends with as many people as you want, but you can only be close friends with very few people. These few people account for 20% or so of your overall relationships, but they make for more or less 80% of the value you get from relationships.
For every relationship you form, there are five attributes that determine if the relationship will work or not. They include mutual enjoyment of each other’s company, respect, shared experience, reciprocity, and trust.

### The live lifestyle you’ve always dreamed of

To live the life of your dreams, you need to know exactly WHAT it looks like, make an exhaustive list of WHAT you want out of your life, next WHAT it takes to get there, and WHAT you need to do that you are not doing. Success always has a blueprint.
Now bring 80 / 20 principles to bring in balance. If you are progressing at work but failing maritally, then you need to address that quickly. If your health is suffering, pause and focus on it. The blueprint you have created will help here.
Don’t live a lopsided life. Let each area of your life be healthy. That’s how to truly be a happy person and if it’s possible for others, it’s possible for you too.
The 80 / 20 principle advocates focusing on the top 20% that brings the most results. While neglecting - or paying less attention to the bottom 80%.The 80 / 20 principle is the **new and nature’s** way of balancing itself.
